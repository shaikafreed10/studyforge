EXPLANATION (< 40 words)

You multiplied 3 by x to get 3x, but forgot to also multiply 3 by 2. The coefficient outside the bracket must distribute to every term inside.

💡 Socratic Hint (Try thinking about this first):

If you have 3 bags containing (x + 2) coins each, how many single coins do you have altogether from all 3 bags?

Visualizing the Area Model for Distribution

3 ×
3 · x = 3x ✓ Computed correctly
3 · 2 = 6 ⚠️ You wrote 2 instead of 6!
Total Area = 3x + 6 Not 3x + 2

Remember: a(b + c) = a·b + a·c. Both terms inside parentheses must be multiplied by the factor outside.
